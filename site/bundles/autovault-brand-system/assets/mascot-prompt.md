# Mascot Prompt Direction

Use this file when the user wants a friendly AutoVault mascot, illustrated
marketing asset, social image, or video character. The mascot may reference the
checkout-logo draft if the project supplies it, but the draft PNG is not a
canonical logo or required input.

## Core Prompt

Friendly AutoVault mascot based on a rounded vault body, charcoal metal casing,
mint `#5ad6c0` trim, center dial lock, two short bottom stubs, small expressive
face integrated into the vault front, simple side handle, soft studio lighting,
clean product illustration, modern SaaS visual language, confident and helpful,
no generated text inside the image, no alternate logo, no parent-brand lockup.

## Optional Context Additions

- For onboarding: the mascot presents a reviewed skill bundle with a mint scan
  line and a calm admitted-state glow.
- For checkout: the mascot is cropped as a square raster logo with high
  legibility at small sizes and no animation assumption.
- For video: the mascot scans, admits, and settles in a three-beat sequence;
  dial motion and mint glow carry most of the animation.
- For docs: the mascot sits near real UI, manifests, or signed bundle imagery
  rather than floating in an abstract scene.

## Negative Prompt

No unrelated parent-brand text, no unreadable generated lettering, no generic robot
helmet, no crypto padlock cliche, no excessive neon, no unrelated creature
features, no complex hands that distract from the vault silhouette, no bright
white background unless the layout requires it.

## Art Direction Checklist

- The vault silhouette is still visible without reading text.
- Mint is used as signal trim, glow, or scan line, not as the entire body color.
- The dial is readable and communicates locked/admitted state.
- The face is friendly but not childish.
- Generated text is avoided or removed in layout.
- The final raster is inspected at the real target size.
