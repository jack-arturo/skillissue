Why

Several tools can talk to one Unity project. That does not make parallel mutation a good idea.

How

Name one writer, route tasks by the surface that can prove them, protect paid operations and credentials, and hand off only after imports and tests settle. A little traffic control beats a prefab merge autopsy.

Related

Use Unity Quest Build for device delivery and Quest Passthrough Camera Capture for a concrete camera pipeline.
