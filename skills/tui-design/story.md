## Why

Terminal interfaces are compact by necessity, not by excuse. A dense screen can still be calm.

## How

Choose a layout that matches the task, make focus and shortcuts predictable, then use color and motion as information instead of wallpaper. The reference catalog is there for the awkward decisions.
